# بطاقة النموذج | Model Card

READY_FOR_REVIEW — جودة التفسير تحتاج مراجعة بشرية، وليست درجة آلية.
## الغرض والاستخدام | Purpose and use
This is for a fictional training exercise using synthetic Tamweel Lite data. A 1 means a simulated request for human review; a 0 is not a safety guarantee or financing approval. I would not use it to score real people or make real loan approval, rejection, pricing, or eligibility decisions. The data are synthetic, the loss units are educational, and the model has not been certified as fair.
بيانات Tamweel Lite اصطناعية؛ الهدف حدث خلال 90 يومًا بعد الطلب. 22 خاصية متاحة وقت الطلب؛ لا معرفات أو تواريخ أو هدف في المدخلات. الاستخدام التعليمي فقط؛ لا قرارات تمويل فعلية.
## البيانات والتحقق | Data and validation
حوض التدريب والاختيار: 6576 طلبًا. المعايرة: 836 طلبًا و78 موجبًا. حجز عملاء المعايرة، 90 يومًا لنضج التسميات، وOOF أمامي متداخل مع فصل العملاء في المستويين. طيات المقارنة: 2023Q1 و2023Q3 و2024Q1. نستبعد النتائج التي لم تنضج قبل الأدوار التالية؛ آخر بيانات التدريب لا تستخدم تلقائيًا.
I excluded the leaky days_past_due_60 field. The nested forward OOF comparison used 2,155 predictions from three later-period folds. Training rows were old enough for their 90-day labels to mature, validation customers were kept out of training, and ensemble weights and the stacker were learned from inner OOF predictions only. There were no outer OOF predictions for the warm-up rows, so 2,155 of the 6,576 fit-and-selection rows were evaluated. There were only three folds, and their training histories overlap. The fold SD of about 0.03 is similar to the differences between the top models; it is descriptive, not a confidence interval. The threshold and loss were also selected on these OOF predictions, so they are development results, not an independent final test. The data are synthetic, and the course had used the training data earlier.
## النموذج والقرار | Model selection
KEEP SINGLE / Logistic. انحراف AP المرجعي: 0.029808. ارجع إلى artifacts/ensemble_comparison.csv وday5_fold_scores.csv للأرقام الكاملة.
I kept the single Logistic Regression model. It had the highest mean AP across the three forward folds: 0.392 (fold SD 0.030), compared with 0.389 for the weighted average, 0.383 for stacking, and 0.372 for equal averaging. The ensembles' AP lifts over Logistic were zero or negative, and none passed the worth-it gate. The differences are also much smaller than the fold SD, so I can't claim an ensemble is better. Logistic's Brier score was 0.0633 and ECE was 0.0188. Stacking had a worse ECE of 0.0311; the weighted average's ECE was slightly lower at 0.0177, but it still had no AP gain. The ensembles require more models and, for stacking, a meta-model, so the extra complexity wasn't justified.
## المعايرة | Calibration
Sigmoid على عينة محجوزة من التدريب والاختيار؛ الرسم والمقاييس تشخيص على عينة تعلم المعاير، وليسا اختبارًا مستقلاً. لا ادعاء بتحسن على تحدٍّ مجهول التسميات.
The sigmoid mapping was fitted using 836 reserved calibration rows, including 78 positives from July–September 2024. The reported scores are on those same rows, so they are fit diagnostics, not an independent test. AP stayed at 0.2878 and ROC-AUC at 0.7890, as expected for a monotonic mapping. The other scores got worse: Brier changed from 0.0765 to 0.0781, ECE from 0.0211 to 0.0349, and log loss from 0.2665 to 0.2773. So these results don't show that calibration improved. I would need separate labeled data to evaluate calibration independently. Challenge labels are unavailable.
## السياسة والسعة والمناطق | Policy and regions
خسارة 10 FN + FP، عتبة OOF الخام 0.16892161427109176 والمنقولة 0.12225843144286948. سعة الدفعة 300؛ المرشحون 330؛ الإشارات النهائية 300. كتلة الدرجات المتساوية لا تقسم. 1=إشارة مراجعة تعليمية، 0=عدم رفع الإشارة.
The OOF-selected raw threshold was 0.1689. It flagged 245 of 2,155 requests (11.4%); the highest period-level flag fraction was 11.7%, within the 12% limit. The threshold's OOF loss was 1,111 fictional teaching units. The regional OOF false-positive rates were Central 7.98% (489 negatives), Western 10.29% (486), Eastern 6.31% (507), and Other 8.10% (494). That is a 3.98 percentage-point gap, with recall ranging from 44.7% in Western to 50.0% in Central. For the full challenge batch, the transported threshold was 0.1223: 330 of 2,500 requests were above it, so the 300-request cap removed 30 flags. The boundary score was 0.1299, and equal-score blocks were not split. The regional results are descriptive only: there are no confidence intervals and they are not a fairness certification. Challenge labels are unavailable, so I can't calculate challenge false-positive rates or check the regional mix of actual errors.
## التفسير وحدوده | Explanation scope
تفسير اليوم الرابع يخص نموذج اليوم الرابع؛ لا يُنسب تلقائيًا إلى هذه النسخة. تغيير النموذج أو خصائصه أو معايرته يستلزم مراجعة التفسير.
No. Day 4's SHAP, permutation importance, reason codes, and review zone explain the class-weighted LightGBM model (80 trees), not the final Day 5 Logistic Regression model. In Day 4, “weighted” referred to class weights; in Day 5, the weighted average is an ensemble. I can't carry Day 4's explanations over as claims about the final model. I would recheck global importance and local reason codes using the final model, then check explanations around the new calibrated threshold of 0.1223.
## المتابعة والقيود | Monitoring and limitations
I would monitor changes in key inputs such as bureau_score and dti, missing values, and the score distribution. As 90-day labels mature, I would check AP by period, Brier, ECE, and observed default rates by probability bin. I would also track how many requests exceed the 0.1223 threshold, how many the 300-request cap removes, and regional flag and false-positive rates with their denominators. The Western–Eastern false-positive gap in this run was 3.98 percentage points, so I would watch whether it persists. I would set review or retraining triggers in advance and investigate them using labeled data, rather than judging performance from the unlabeled challenge set.
OOF يستخدم للاختيار، وثلاث فترات ليست اختبار دلالة. العتبة قد تتغير سعتها عند نقلها إلى نموذج معاد التدريب. لا تسميات للتحدي، ولا مقاييس أداء أو شهادة عدالة له. البيانات لا تمثل أشخاصًا أو مناطق حقيقية.
